## AUDIENCE & SOURCES

Local conditions to check the advice against: PAGASA signals, barangay realities, local products and peso prices, wet-season conditions.

Safety-critical domains that commonly need verification: water-treatment ratios (e.g. bleach per litre), medicine storage temperatures, first-aid/medical instructions, disease symptoms (leptospirosis, dengue), electrical/generator safety, structural/evacuation advice, food safety, and PAGASA wind-signal actions.

Authoritative sources to verify against: DOH, PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, Philippine Red Cross.
